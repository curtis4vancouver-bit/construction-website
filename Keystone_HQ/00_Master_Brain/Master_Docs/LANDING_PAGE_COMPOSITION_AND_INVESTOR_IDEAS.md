### **Section-by-Section Blueprint**

#### **Section 1: The Hero Header (Sweat-Equity Proposition)**
*   **Visual Background:** High-contrast photo of a custom modern home foundation anchored to a steep rock slope in Squamish.
*   **Headline:** `100% PASSIVE REAL ESTATE INVESTING`
*   **Subheadline:** `You fund the project. We manage the permits, civil work, and construction. Get your principal back first, plus 30% of the profits. Zero management required.`
*   **CTA Button:** `[Download BC JV Financial Model]`

#### **Section 2: The Payback Sequence (Security First)**
*   **Copy Angle:** Illustrate the waterfall return model to build investor safety:
    *   *Step 1:* Investor puts up $1.5M.
    *   *Step 2:* Keystone builds the custom home.
    *   *Step 3:* Property sells for $2.5M.
    *   *Step 4:* **Investor is paid back their entire $1.5M first.**
    *   *Step 5:* The remaining $1.0M profit is split: **$700,000 to Wayne (70%)** and **$300,000 to the Investor (30%)**.

*   **BC Hydro Registered Civil Contractor:** We manage excavation and grid connections directly, saving $50k-$100k in markups and slashing timeline delays.
*   **BC Builder License 52603:** Full licensing to unlock 2-10 home warranty approvals instantly.
*   **BC Bill 44 Triplex Specialization:** We multiply land equity by turning single-family lots into high-density multiplex developments under BC's new zoning laws.

#### **Section 4: The Live Project Tracker (Eliminating Investor Anxiety)**
*   **Copy Angle:** Introduce the **Possibilities Client Portal**. Show a mockup screenshot of the private dashboard where investors log in to track daily budgets, see real-time bank draw details, and view site superintendent photo feeds.
