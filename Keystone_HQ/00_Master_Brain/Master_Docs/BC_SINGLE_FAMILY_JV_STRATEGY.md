## 1. Overview: The Silent Investor Equity Partnership

This Joint Venture (JV) model is designed for custom single-family home developments across British Columbia (Squamish, Whistler, West Vancouver, and North Vancouver). 

Under this structure, Keystone Possibilities partners with a **Silent Capital Partner (Investor)** to fund and build premium custom homes. Keystone manages 100% of the operational and construction lifecycle, delivering high-performance, Net-Zero ready properties while the investor provides passive funding.

## 2. The Deal & Profit-Sharing Structure (70/30 Sweat-Equity Split)

The deal features a developer-favorable split based on the extensive value, licensing, and management Wayne Stevenson provides:

*   **Silent Capital Partner (Investor):**
    *   **Contribution:** Invests **100% of the capital** required for land acquisition and construction (typically **$1,000,000 to $1,500,000** depending on the lot and build spec).
    *   **Principal Payback:** Receives **100% of their initial principal investment back first** upon property sale or refinancing.
    *   **Profit Split:** Receives **30% of the net profits** generated by the development.
*   **Managing Partner (Wayne Stevenson / Keystone Possibilities):**
    *   **Contribution:** Manages 100% of the operation. Slices through municipal bureaucracy (BC Bill 44 multiplex permits), acts as General Contractor, manages all excavation/civil works as a Registered BC Hydro Civil Contractor, and executes the complete design and build.
    *   **Profit Split:** Receives **70% of the net profits** generated by the development.

### Example Deal Mechanics ($1.5M Capital Investment)
1.  **Funding:** The Silent Partner invests $1,500,000 in cash to fund the land acquisition and construction.
2.  **Execution:** Wayne Stevenson manages the entire development, building a luxury custom home.
3.  **Exit:** The completed residence sells for $2,500,000.
4.  **Distribution Sequence:**
    *   **Step 1 (Principal Return):** The Silent Partner is paid back their initial **$1,500,000** investment.
    *   **Step 2 (Profit Calculation):** Net Profit = $2,500,000 (Sale) - $1,500,000 (Cost) = **$1,000,000**.
    *   **Step 3 (Profit Split):**
        *   **Wayne (70%):** Receives **$700,000** for running the project.
        *   **Silent Partner (30%):** Receives **$300,000** (total return = $1,800,000).

## 3. The Build Specifications (Keystone Standard)

Every single-family JV home is built to the high-performance Keystone standard, ensuring rapid sales velocity and maximum valuation:
*   **Step Code Compliance:** Built to BC Energy Step Code Tier 4 or 5 (Net-Zero Ready), minimizing utility costs and maximizing municipal permit speed under BC Bill 44.
*   **Civil Infrastructure:** Excavation and underground electrical wiring are managed directly by Keystone as a BC Hydro Registered Civil Contractor, avoiding sub-contractor markups and scheduling delays.
*   **Biophilic Design:** High-ceiling, concrete-and-glass architecture that maximizes natural Sea-to-Sky light and thermal efficiency.
