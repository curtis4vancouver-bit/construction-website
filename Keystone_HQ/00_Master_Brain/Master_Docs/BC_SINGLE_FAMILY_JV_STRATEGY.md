## 1. Overview: The BC Single-Family Equity Partnership

To complement our international longevity retreat developments, Keystone Possibilities is launching a localized **Single-Family Residential Joint Venture (JV) Program** across British Columbia (focusing on Squamish, Whistler, West Vancouver, and North Vancouver). 

This program targets landowners, lot finders, and local partners who control prime residential lots or teardown properties but lack the liquid capital, BC Builder licensing, or civil engineering expertise to build.

---

## 2. The Deal & Profit-Sharing Structure (70/30 Capital Split)

We operate on a transparent, developer-funded equity split designed to align incentives and maximize profit on exit:

*   **Capital & Build Partner (Wayne / Keystone Possibilities):**
    *   **Contribution:** Funds **100% of the capital** required for land acquisition and construction. Puts down the cash, handles the construction loan financing, and executes the complete build (site excavation, BC Hydro civil connections, framing, finishing).
    *   **Profit Split:** Receives **70% of net profits** upon property sale or refinance.
*   **Land or Sourcing Partner (Landowner / Finder):**
    *   **Contribution:** Provides the lot (either contributes existing land equity or sources a off-market distress property/teardown).
    *   **Profit Split:** Receives **30% of net profits** upon property sale or refinance.

### Example Deal Mechanics (Squamish Teardown)
1.  **Acquisition:** Landowner contributes a lot valued at $1,000,000 (debt-free or with minimal mortgage). Alternatively, a finder locates an off-market lot, and Wayne funds the $1,000,000 purchase.
2.  **Construction:** Wayne funds 100% of construction costs ($800,000) to build a premium custom home utilizing Step Code Tier 4/5 specifications.
3.  **Sale:** The completed luxury residence sells for $2,600,000.
4.  **Profit Distribution:** 
    *   Total Sales Revenue: $2,600,000
    *   Less Acquisition & Build Costs: $1,800,000
    *   Net Profit: $800,000
    *   **Wayne (70%):** $560,000
    *   **Partner (30%):** $240,000

## 3. The Build Specifications (Keystone Standard)

Every single-family JV home is built to the high-performance Keystone standard, ensuring rapid sales velocity and maximum valuation:
*   **Step Code Compliance:** Built to BC Energy Step Code Tier 4 or 5 (Net-Zero Ready), minimizing utility costs and maximizing municipal permit speed under BC Bill 44.
*   **Civil Infrastructure:** Excavation and underground electrical wiring are managed directly by Keystone as a BC Hydro Registered Civil Contractor, avoiding sub-contractor markups and scheduling delays.
*   **Biophilic Design:** High-ceiling, concrete-and-glass architecture that maximizes natural Sea-to-Sky light and thermal efficiency.
