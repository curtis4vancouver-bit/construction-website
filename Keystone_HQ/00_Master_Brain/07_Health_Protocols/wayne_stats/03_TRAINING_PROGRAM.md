## 1. Endocrinological Architecture and Peptide Pharmacokinetics

To override standard biological energy partitioning that prevents simultaneous fat loss and muscle accretion, this protocol coordinates two exogenous peptides:

### A. Tirzepatide (7.5mg every 5 days / 120-hour cycle)
*   **Tmax (Hours 8 - 72 post-injection):** Peak plasma concentrations. Gastric emptying is severely delayed; appetite suppression is centralized; glycogen utilization is reduced. Central nervous system (CNS) energy levels may dip. Heavy axial spinal loading is contraindicated.
*   **Trough (Days 4 & 5):** Serum concentrations decline. Gastric emptying normalizes, permitting higher carbohydrate absorption and maximal CNS output. Heavy compound strength sessions are programmed here.
*   **The rolling 5-Day Biological Cycle:** The training split operates on a rolling 5-day cycle matching the drug's decay curve, rather than a rigid 7-day calendar.

### B. CJC-1295 with DAC (75mcg daily / 3 units)
*   **Mechanism:** Formulated with amino acid substitutions resisting DPP-4 degradation and the Drug Affinity Complex (DAC) binding to serum albumin. Extends half-life to 6-8 days. Daily microdosing maintains a stable elevation of IGF-1 and growth hormone without the joint pain or insulin resistance of large weekly bolus injections.
*   **Trello Washout Constraint:** Daily subcutaneous microdosing of 3 units (75mcg) continues until the designated cessation date on the Trello calendar, at which point all administration stops immediately to allow pituitary receptor resensitization.

## 2. The Biologically Synced 5-Day Training Architecture

To ensure CNS recovery, the program enforces **two complete rest days per calendar week** (Thursday and Sunday) which supersede the biological M-Day training rotation.

### The 5-Day Biological Exertion Cycle
1.  **M-Day 1 (Injection Day): Active Recovery and Systemic Flush**
    *   *Prescription:* Zone 2 Cardio (conversational pace, 60-70% max HR), core stability, and deep mobility/stretching.
    *   *Goal:* Clear lactic acid and stimulate mitochondrial biogenesis without CNS fatigue.
2.  **M-Day 2 (Peak Suppressed / Low Energy): Upper Body Hypertrophy (Push Focus)**
    *   *Prescription:* Moderate load (65-75% 1RM), high volume (10-15 reps). Supported pushing movements.
    *   *Exercises:* Machine Chest Press, DB Incline Press, Lateral Cable Raises, Overhead Machine Press, Tricep Pushdowns.
3.  **M-Day 3 (Stabilizing): Lower Body Hypertrophy (Quad/Calf Focus)**
    *   *Prescription:* High-rep isolation and machine-supported leg movements to protect the spine.
    *   *Exercises:* Leg Press (high feet), Hack Squats, Leg Extensions (slow 3-sec negatives), Seated Calf Raises.
4.  **M-Day 4 (Energy Rising): Heavy Upper Body Strength (Pull/Back Focus)**
    *   *Prescription:* High load (80-90% 1RM), low reps (4-6 reps).
    *   *Exercises:* Weighted Pull-ups/Lat Pulldowns, Barbell Rows, Face Pulls, Heavy Shrugs.
5.  **M-Day 5 (Energy Peak): Heavy Lower Body Strength (Posterior Chain Focus)**
    *   *Prescription:* Heavy compound axial loading at the pharmacokinetic trough.
    *   *Exercises:* Conventional Deadlifts, Romanian Deadlifts (RDLs), Heavy Low-Bar Back Squats, Hanging Leg Raises.
